### The Peach Proxy

I am actively building a [macOS-native MiTM proxy](https://thepeachproxy.com) in Swift that sits somewhere between a regular proxy like
[Charles](https://www.charlesproxy.com/) or [mitmproxy](https://www.mitmproxy.org/), and a security researcher's Swiss army knife like
Burp Suite.
